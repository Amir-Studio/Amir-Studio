PROJECT CASE STUDY / Luxury retail

Swiss Gallery

A digital presentation for luxury watches, focused on imagery, clarity and a considered product-browsing experience.

Luxury retail · Digital experience2026AMIRSTUDIO
01 / THE BRIEF

Starting with the brief.

The project aimed to present premium products with a clear visual hierarchy, keeping product imagery central while making essential information easy to find.

02 / THE APPROACH

Design with intention.

The design uses a restrained palette, considered spacing and a product-led layout. Typography and interface details support the brand character without competing with it.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

Built around the details.

The result is a focused retail experience that brings product imagery, descriptive information and browsing structure together in a coherent interface.
