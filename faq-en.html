Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to common questions about design, development and the way we work together.

Clarity before we begin01 — 10
01 How do I start a project with AmirStudio? +

Send a short introduction to your business, your website goals and what you would like to build through the contact form or email. If you have references or existing materials, include their links. We can then review the initial brief and discuss the next steps.

02 How is the project cost determined? +

Pricing depends on scope, page count, custom functionality, design complexity, third-party integrations and the time required. A tailored estimate is prepared after the requirements are understood; we do not quote a fixed price without knowing the project details.

03 How long does a website take to build? +

Timing depends on project size and complexity, as well as how quickly content, imagery and feedback are provided. Once the scope is agreed, we outline realistic milestones and a delivery schedule.

04 Will the website work on mobile and tablet? +

Yes. Responsive design is part of the design and development process. Layouts, imagery, navigation and forms are considered for different screen sizes and should be checked at common viewport widths.

05 Do you use ready-made templates? +

The visual direction and structure are shaped around each business and its needs. Using a technical tool or library does not mean imposing a generic appearance; design decisions should remain specific to the project.

06 Can I request changes during the project? +

Yes. Reviews and feedback are included at agreed stages. The number and scope of revision rounds are defined in the project proposal. Major changes beyond the original scope may require additional time and a separate estimate.

07 Do you include SEO? +

Technical foundations such as semantic structure, page titles, meta descriptions, responsive layouts and performance considerations can be included. Search rankings depend on many factors and ongoing work; no specific position or result can be guaranteed.

08 Do I need to provide a domain and hosting? +

We can help identify suitable domain and hosting options. Ideally, primary accounts and ownership should remain in the business owner's name. Domain, hosting and third-party service costs are separate unless explicitly included in the proposal.

09 Is ongoing support available after launch? +

Support, technical fixes, content updates and future enhancements can be arranged. The scope, duration and cost of ongoing support should be agreed separately and clearly.

10 How can I ask about my own project? +

Email projects.amirstudio@gmail.com or contact us through Instagram. A clear initial description of your goals helps us give a more useful response.
