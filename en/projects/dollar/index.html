Dollar Market Dashboard

A Persian-first dashboard that brings reference prices, historical charts and supporting analysis into one interface.

Market data · Financial dashboard2026AMIRSTUDIO
01 / THE BRIEF

Starting with the brief.

The product is designed to make market information easier to view, compare and understand, while distinguishing recorded data from statistical estimates.

02 / THE APPROACH

Design with intention.

A deep-black interface, restrained green and gold accents, chart-first layout and compact market cards give the dashboard a focused analytical character.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

Built around the details.

A responsive market-viewing experience that brings reference prices, historical movement and supporting context together.
