02Website Design & Development Services10 questions+
01What types of websites can AmirStudio create?+

Depending on the brief, projects may include business websites, service websites, portfolios, landing pages, online catalogues, e-commerce sites and custom web experiences.

02What does custom website design mean?+

The structure, visual language and interactions are shaped around a specific business and its audience instead of simply applying a generic theme.

03Can a website be designed from scratch?+

Yes. The content structure, page layouts and visual direction can be defined for the project. Technical choices are then made according to its requirements and future maintenance.

04Can you build a multi-page website?+

Yes. Pages such as Home, About, Services, Work, Contact and project-specific sections can be planned around the user's journey and the amount of content.

05Will my homepage be designed specifically for my business?+

The homepage should quickly explain what the business offers, who it serves and where visitors should go next. Its layout is planned around those priorities.

06Can you build a one-page website?+

A one-page website can work well for a focused service, campaign or simple introduction. It is suitable when the content and user journey do not require separate pages.

07Can custom features be added to my website?+

Custom forms, filters, content interactions, dashboards and third-party integrations can be considered after their purpose, technical requirements and scope are reviewed.

08Will the website match my brand identity?+

The visual system should reflect the brand through typography, colour, imagery, tone and interface details, creating a consistent experience across digital touchpoints.

09Can AmirStudio redesign an existing website?+

Yes. The current site's structure, usability, visual design and technical issues can be reviewed before deciding whether a full redesign or focused improvements make more sense.

10What should I prepare before requesting a website?+

A short business description, audience, website goals, essential features and a few visual references are enough to begin an initial discussion.

03Website Design Costs & Pricing10 questions+
01How much does a website cost at AmirStudio?+

There is no single price for every project. Cost depends on page count, design complexity, custom functionality, content needs, integrations and development time.

02How is a website design quote calculated?+

After the requirements are understood, the work is divided into clear deliverables such as discovery, design, development, testing and launch preparation.

03Why do website design prices vary so much?+

Websites differ in their content, number of templates, interactions, integrations and technical requirements. A useful comparison must consider the scope, not only the headline price.

04Will I receive a project estimate before work begins?+

Once the brief is clear, the proposed scope, deliverables, schedule and fee can be documented so expectations are aligned before development starts.

05Can a website be built in phases?+

Where appropriate, the project can begin with a focused first release and expand later with additional pages or features. The initial architecture should account for likely future needs.

06Are domain and hosting costs included?+

Domain registration, hosting and third-party subscriptions are separate unless the proposal explicitly includes them.

07Do major changes after design approval cost extra?+

Feedback within the agreed review process is handled as specified in the proposal. New features or substantial changes outside the original scope may require a revised estimate.

08Is the cheapest website always the best option?+

Not necessarily. Consider whether the site meets your goals, how well it is built, how easy it is to maintain and what future changes may cost.

09What information helps you estimate my website project?+

Share the business type, website goal, approximate page count, required features and any references. It is fine if some details are not decided yet.

10Does an e-commerce website cost more than a business website?+

It often does because product management, search and filtering, cart, checkout and order workflows add complexity. The final cost depends on the actual requirements.

01What are the main stages of a website project?+

A typical process includes discovery, scope definition, content planning, visual design, development, testing, revisions and launch. The sequence is adjusted to the project.

02What is the first step in building a website?+

Start by defining the audience and the main purpose of the website. Those decisions guide the page structure, content priorities and required features.

03Do you plan page layouts before coding?+

For projects requiring a considered visual direction, the structure and design are reviewed before implementation so development follows clear decisions.

04Can I review progress during development?+

Review points and the way previews are shared are agreed at the start. Timely feedback helps keep the project aligned with its intended scope.

05When should I prepare website copy and images?+

Ideally, core content and imagery are gathered during planning. If they are not ready, a content checklist can clarify what is needed and when.

06Will the website be tested before launch?+

Key links, forms, responsive layouts and general functionality should be checked before publication. The test plan depends on the features included.

07Can I see a preview before the website goes live?+

Where a staging or preview environment is available, it can be shared for review. Access arrangements are agreed as part of the project workflow.

08Who publishes the finished website?+

Launch is handled according to the agreed access and hosting setup. Primary domain and service accounts should ideally remain under the business owner's control.

09What happens if my requirements change during the project?+

The impact on scope, schedule and cost is reviewed first. If both sides agree, the project brief can be updated before the additional work proceeds.

10What do I receive when the website is delivered?+

Deliverables may include the agreed website files, access details, usage guidance and relevant documentation. The exact handover is listed in the project scope.

05Custom Design, UX & UI10 questions+
01What is the difference between custom design and a website template?+

Custom design shapes the structure and experience around the business. A template starts from a predefined system that may limit distinctive layouts or specialised requirements.

02What do UX and UI mean in website design?+

UX describes the overall experience and user journey. UI refers to the visible and interactive elements such as navigation, buttons, forms, typography and layout.

03Why does user experience matter?+

Visitors should be able to understand the content and complete important tasks without confusion. Good UX makes paths clear and interactions predictable.

04Should a website follow my existing brand identity?+

Yes, where a brand system exists. Typography, colour, imagery and tone should feel consistent with the business while remaining readable and useful.

05Is minimalist website design right for every business?+

Not always. Simplicity is useful when it makes information clearer. The right visual approach depends on the audience, content and purpose of the website.

06How can website navigation be made easier to use?+

Prioritise the most important pages, use familiar labels and avoid unnecessary choices. Navigation should reflect what visitors are actually trying to find.

07Can AmirStudio design right-to-left websites?+

Yes. Persian and other RTL experiences require deliberate handling of text direction, layout, typography and interface behaviour from the beginning.

08Can you create a bilingual website?+

Yes. Each language needs appropriate content, clear URL paths and links to its equivalent version. Translation should be reviewed for meaning and natural phrasing.

09Why are speed and simplicity important to UX?+

A responsive, lightweight page helps visitors reach information with less friction. Visual design and technical performance should be considered together.

10Can I share design references and preferences?+

Absolutely. References help explain the desired direction. They are evaluated against your brand, audience and functional goals rather than copied without context.

06SEO & Google Visibility10 questions+
01Does AmirStudio build websites with SEO in mind?+

The technical foundations can include semantic HTML, clear page titles, meta descriptions, crawlable links, responsive layouts and performance considerations.

02Can AmirStudio guarantee the number one Google ranking?+

No. No designer or developer can guarantee a specific ranking. Visibility depends on competition, useful content, authority, technical health and ongoing SEO work.

03Do you include relevant keywords in website content?+

Relevant search phrases can be incorporated naturally into headings, page copy and metadata. Repeating keywords unnaturally can harm readability and user experience.

04What is technical SEO for a new website?+

It covers elements such as crawlability, indexability, canonical URLs, XML sitemaps, structured data where appropriate and checks for technical errors.

05Should every page have a unique title and meta description?+

Important pages should have distinct, accurate titles and descriptions that explain their content and help users understand the result in search.

06Can my website be connected to Google Search Console?+

With the required ownership access, the site can be verified in Search Console to monitor indexing, search performance and reported issues.

07Does website speed affect SEO?+

Page experience and performance are part of technical quality. They should be improved alongside useful content and strong relevance to the search query.

08Do images need alt text?+

Meaningful images should have concise, descriptive alternative text. Decorative images can use empty alt attributes so assistive technology can skip them.

09Can a blog help my website attract search traffic?+

Useful articles that answer real audience questions can create additional discovery paths. They should be relevant, accurate and maintained rather than published only to add keywords.

10What should I do after launch to improve Google rankings?+

Publish helpful content, monitor Search Console, fix technical issues and earn relevant mentions naturally. SEO is an ongoing process, not a one-time launch task.

07E-commerce & Business Websites10 questions+
01Can AmirStudio design an e-commerce website?+

Yes. The project begins by reviewing product volume, ordering, payment, inventory and customer experience before selecting an appropriate implementation.

02What features does an online store usually need?+

Common features include product pages, categories, search, filtering, cart, checkout and order information. The exact setup depends on the business model.

03Can product pages have a custom layout?+

Yes. Product details, imagery, specifications and calls to action can be organised around the information customers need to make a decision.

04Can you build a website for a company or professional service?+

Yes. A business website can present the company, services, strengths, selected work and contact options in a clear, credible structure.

05Which pages should a company website include?+

Home, About, Services, Work or Case Studies and Contact are common starting points. The final structure should reflect the audience and sales process.

06Can you create an online product catalogue without checkout?+

Yes. A catalogue can present products with categories, images and specifications without requiring online payment or a full shopping cart.

07Can a service website include an enquiry form?+

Yes. A form can collect the information needed for a useful follow-up. It should request only necessary details and provide clear confirmation.

08Will an online store be optimised for mobile devices?+

Product browsing, filters, forms and checkout steps should be designed and tested for smaller screens as well as desktop layouts.

09Can an e-commerce website grow with my business?+

A suitable architecture can make future improvements easier, although every new feature should be reviewed for technical impact and maintenance needs.

10How do I choose between a business website and an online store?+

If the main goal is to explain services and receive enquiries, a business website may be enough. If customers need to purchase online, e-commerce should be considered.

08Technology, Responsive Design & Security10 questions+
01Will my website work on mobile phones?+

Responsive layouts are considered across common screen sizes. Navigation, images, typography and forms should be checked on phones, tablets and desktop screens.

02What is responsive web design?+

Responsive design adapts page layouts and interface elements to different viewport sizes so the website remains readable and usable across devices.

03Which technologies are used to build websites?+

The stack depends on the project. HTML, CSS and JavaScript are core web technologies; additional frameworks or services are selected only when they serve a clear need.

04Can you build a website without WordPress?+

Yes. A project may use custom development or a content management system. The choice should reflect editing needs, control, budget and long-term maintenance.

05Can AmirStudio develop a web application?+

For projects beyond informational pages, the required workflows and application features can be assessed before defining a realistic development scope.

06How is website security considered?+

Security practices should match the stack and data involved. Common foundations include HTTPS, input validation, controlled access and maintaining software dependencies.

07How should website forms handle user information?+

Forms should collect only necessary data. Transmission and storage need to be considered in relation to the services used and the sensitivity of the information.

08Can my website use a custom domain?+

Yes. Once a domain is registered, DNS settings can be configured to point it to the website. The business owner should retain control of the domain account.

09Can a website be hosted on GitHub Pages?+

GitHub Pages can suit static websites that do not require server-side processing. Projects needing a backend or database require an appropriate additional service.

10Can the website be expanded after launch?+

Yes. Clear structure and maintainable implementation make it easier to add pages and features later, subject to technical review and planning.

09Website Content, Images & Languages10 questions+
01Who writes the text for my website?+

Content responsibilities should be defined in the project scope. AmirStudio can help identify the structure and information each page needs.

02Do I need professional photography for my website?+

Relevant, high-quality imagery can strengthen the brand experience. The type of photography depends on the business and the role images play on each page.

03Can I use photos and assets I already have?+

Existing assets can be used when their quality, usage rights and visual fit are suitable for the design.

04Can my website be available in English and Persian?+

Yes. Each language should have appropriate content, clear page paths and links to equivalent pages. Machine translation should be reviewed before publication.

05Does typography matter in website design?+

Yes. Font choice, sizing, line length and spacing affect readability and the character of the brand. These details should work together across devices.

06How should I write the homepage copy?+

Explain what the business offers, who it helps and what problem it solves. Then present the main message, credible proof and a clear next step.

07Can I update text and images after launch?+

That depends on the implementation and editing tools. If independent content updates are important, the project should include a suitable content workflow.

08Should portfolio projects include written descriptions?+

Short case studies can explain the goal, challenge, contribution and verifiable outcome. This gives visitors more context than screenshots alone.

09How can large images be prevented from slowing down a website?+

Use appropriately sized images, compression, modern formats where supported and lazy loading for below-the-fold media.

10Should website content be written for search engines?+

Content should first answer visitors' questions. Clear headings, accurate language and relevant terminology then help search engines understand the page.

10Support, Maintenance & Future Development10 questions+
01Is support available after my website launches?+

Support can be arranged by agreement. The scope, duration, response expectations and fee should be stated clearly.

02What if an issue appears after launch?+

The issue should be reviewed in context. Responsibilities for fixing defects within the original scope and for ongoing support should be defined in the agreement.

03Can I manage website content myself?+

That depends on the technical setup. If independent editing is required, an appropriate content management tool or workflow should be planned from the start.

04Can new pages be added after delivery?+

Yes. New pages can be scoped as follow-up work, with design, content and technical consistency reviewed before implementation.

05How often should a website be updated?+

Software dependencies, content and connected services should be reviewed periodically. The right schedule depends on the site's technology and how frequently it changes.

06Does my website need backups?+

Sites with important data or frequent updates should have a backup and recovery plan. Ownership and backup frequency should be agreed.

07Can the visual design be refreshed later?+

Yes. A refresh may involve small refinements or a full redesign. User feedback and performance data can help decide what needs to change.

08Can my website be moved to another host or domain?+

Migration may be possible depending on the stack and access. Files, databases, DNS settings and third-party dependencies should be reviewed beforehand.

09Who owns the website after delivery?+

The client should retain control of primary domain and service accounts. Ownership of code, design files, licences and third-party assets should be stated in the agreement.

10How do I request future website improvements?+

Send the website address, the change you have in mind and the reason for it through the studio's contact channels.
